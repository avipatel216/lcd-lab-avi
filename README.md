# lcd-lab-avi
LCD display project for IoT lab 11

Project title: IoTlab11-LCD-display

Description: reaction timer game using Arduino IDE, an LCD and a pushbutton

Hardware Setup:

    Connect the LCD module to the Arduino Board:
        RS to Digital Pin 7
        EN to Digital Pin 8
        D4 to Digital Pin 9
        D5 to Digital Pin 10
        D6 to Digital Pin 11
        D7 to Digital Pin 12
        VSS to GND
        VDD to 5V
        VO to 1kΩ resistor to GND
        RW to GND
        A (Backlight +) to 5V
        K (Backlight -) to GND

    Connect the RGB LED (Common Cathode):
     "S" side to Digital Pin 3
     "-" to side to GND
     Middle pin to 5V
